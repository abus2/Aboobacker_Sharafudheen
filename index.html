04.8 / QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Things people ask

Are you available for internships or part-time work?

Yes — I’m open to internships, entry-level roles and small collaborations in finance, accounting and digital marketing. Use the contact form below or save my contact card.

Which tools do you work with?

Accounting with Tally, data and reports in Microsoft Excel, plus digital marketing basics such as content, research and online branding. I also build simple web apps and accounting tools.

Are the finance tools free to use?

Yes. The calculators on the Apps page (GST, EMI, SIP, budget, profit, FD / RD, break-even and percentage) are free and run entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded. They are learning tools, not professional advice.

Can I see your certificates?

Yes — my courses and certificates are listed in the Certificates section above. If you need to verify a qualification, send me a message and I’ll share the details.

How quickly do you reply?

I aim to reply within a couple of days. My local time is shown in the contact section so you know when I’m likely online.
